Who sees the ads

Three kinds of people

New travelers like our past guests

Instagram finds people who look like the guests we already have, using our guest list as the starting point.

New travelers who fit the trip

Adults in the US and Canada interested in luxury travel. For Tented, the ads lean toward parents planning family trips.

People who already showed interest

Anyone who watched most of a video, followed along on Instagram, or visited our site in the last month sees a reminder.

Guests who stayed with us in the past year are left out, so we do not pay to reach people who already know us.

How we will know it works

We count bookings, not likes

Every link is labeled

Each ad and each link our team sends carries a label, so a booking can be traced back to the video that started it.

Every conversation is logged

Our team keeps a simple record of each message: which property, whether it was a real inquiry, and whether it became a booking.

We judge on real results

The main question is simple: for every dollar spent, how much did guests book? Views and likes are only early signals.

The first two months

Launch, learn, then move money to what works

  1. Before launchConnect the accounts, check that every landing page records visits, and set up the welcome message our team uses.
  2. Weeks 1 and 2Let the ads run untouched. Instagram and TikTok need this time to learn who responds.
  3. Week 3Pause the videos that are not pulling their weight and put the held-back $500 behind the best one.
  4. Week 4First read: which route brings more real inquiries and bookings, messages or the landing page.
  5. Month 2Shift money toward the winners, refresh tired videos, and keep counting bookings as they come in. Trips are often booked weeks ahead, so the full picture takes a couple of months.
