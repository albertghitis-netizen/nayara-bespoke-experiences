Paid Social Advertising Campaign Structure

Multi-platform A/B testing strategy for Nayara Resorts: Meta, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and YouTube long-form with landing page and direct DM conversion paths.

Campaign Overview

This structure runs a unified creative asset pool across four platforms, split into two distinct conversion paths: half the budget to dedicated landing pages (measured purchase intent), half to direct message (measured engagement and brand interaction). Each platform gets format-specific creative (vertical for mobile-first platforms, horizontal for YouTube long-form and Facebook desktop) but the same core messaging and offer.

Key principle: The landing page path measures direct booking conversion. The DM path measures lead quality and engagement velocity. Compare cost-per-lead and cost-per-booking across both to determine which surfaces more bookable intent per dollar spent.

Creative Specifications by Format

Vertical Video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)

Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (portrait)
Resolution: 1080 x 1920px minimum
Duration: 15–60 seconds (30 seconds optimal for TikTok)
File Format: MP4, H.264 codec
Max File Size: 500 MB
Frame Rate: 24–60 fps

Vertical video should open with 2–3 seconds of visual hook (often a landscape reveal of the property or sloths, then cut to vertical composition). Text overlays should be centered and legible at thumb-scroll speed. Call-to-action (CTA) placement: appear at the 8–10 second mark and repeat at the end.

Horizontal Video (Facebook Feed, YouTube Long-form)

Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (landscape)
Resolution: 1280 x 720px minimum (1920 x 1080px preferred)
Duration: Facebook feed ads: 15–60 seconds. YouTube long-form: 6–30 seconds (skippable in-stream) or 20–60 seconds (non-skippable).
File Format: MP4, H.264 codec
Max File Size: Facebook 4 GB, YouTube 128 GB
Frame Rate: 24–60 fps

Horizontal video can be the same property footage but composed for the wider canvas. On Facebook, allow 3–5 seconds for slow-loading feeds. On YouTube long-form (TrueView), the first 5 seconds are critical: the viewer can skip, so lead with a question or visual promise.

Platform-Specific Campaign Setup

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Account Structure

Run three campaign tiers under one ad account:

  1. Campaign: One parent campaign per property (Gardens, Springs, Tented). Within each, three ad sets: Landing Page Path (desktop + mobile feed), DM Path (desktop + mobile feed), and iOS Audience (if you have a retargeting list).
  2. Ad Set: Split audiences by conversion intent. Create custom audiences from your email list, website visitors (if GA4 pixel is live), and lookalikes from booking data.
  3. Ad: Two creative variants per placement. Example: Creative A shows sloths (emotional, family-forward); Creative B shows pool/spa (luxury, adults).

Audience Targeting

Audience Segment Age Range Interests Primary Property
Family Travelers 25–55 Family travel, Costa Rica, adventure with kids, eco-tourism Gardens (primary)
Luxury Couples 35–65 Luxury travel, spa, romance, wellness retreats Springs (primary)
Adventure / Wellness 25–50 Glamping, wellness retreats, adventure travel, nature Tented (primary)
Resort Comparison Shoppers 25–60 Luxury Costa Rica resorts, 5-star hotels, all-inclusive All (feeder)

Bidding Strategy

  • Landing Page Path: Use Conversions objective, optimize for "Booking Completed" event (if piped from SynXis). Bid cap: $40–60 per conversion (adjust based on historical AOV).
  • DM Path: Use Messages objective, optimize for "Conversation Started" or "Reply Received" event. Bid cap: $2–5 per message.
  • Flex scaling: Use Meta's dynamic creative optimization to test which ad variants drive lower CPC within the same audience.

Placements

  • Landing page variant: Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Explore (automatic).
  • DM variant: Instagram Stories, Messenger, Instagram Feed (Instagram's native DM prompts).
  • Avoid Reels for DM path (Reels don't natively drive message intent; use Stories or Feed).

TikTok Ads

Campaign Structure

TikTok ad manager runs separate from Meta. Create one parent campaign (e.g., "Nayara 2026 Q4") with three ad groups: Landing Page Conversion, DM Lead, and Retargeting.

Audience Targeting

  • Core interest: Travel, adventure, eco-tourism, luxury lifestyle, family travel.
  • Geo targeting: US, Canada, UK, Mexico (primary markets). Secondary: Australia, Spain.
  • Age range: 18–55 (TikTok skews young; narrow secondary audiences to 25+ for luxury properties).
  • Lookalike: Create lookalike audiences from your email subscriber list and website visitors.

Creative Setup

  • Video format: 9:16 vertical, 15–60 seconds.
  • Scroll-stopping hook: First frame must grab attention in 1 second (visual contrast, movement, or text overlay).
  • Native feel: TikTok penalizes heavily produced ads. Use authentic, user-generated-style content. Frame the video to feel like a native TikTok "For You" post, not a polished commercial.
  • Caption: Use TikTok's native caption tool. Speak directly to the viewer ("POV: you're booking a Costa Rica family trip").
  • Music: Licensed music from TikTok's library, trending sounds recommended. Ensure audio is part of the platform's native experience.

Conversion Tracking

  • Landing page path: TikTok's Pixel tracks your landing page conversion event (purchase, booking started, etc.). URL parameter: ?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=nayara_gardens_family
  • DM path: TikTok has no native DM destination option. Workaround: use landing pages with a prominent DM CTA (e.g., "Message us for exclusive pricing"), then track link clicks in TikTok Ads Manager.

Bidding

  • Landing page: Optimize for web event (Booking Completed). Cost cap: $30–50 per conversion.
  • DM feeder: Optimize for clicks (CTR), let TikTok auto-optimize within budget. Daily budget: $20–50 to start, scale if profitable.

YouTube Shorts Ads

Campaign Structure

YouTube Shorts ads run through Google Ads (same account as YouTube long-form). Create a separate ad group specifically for Shorts placement to separate performance data from long-form in-stream ads.

Creative Requirements

  • Format: 9:16 vertical video, identical to TikTok creative.
  • Duration: 15–60 seconds (auto-loops; shorter is better, 20–30s optimal).
  • Auto-play: YouTube Shorts auto-play muted; include captions or text overlays for all dialogue.
  • CTA card: YouTube Shorts allows a clickable banner CTA at the bottom of the video (appears at 8+ seconds). Use "Learn More" (to landing page) or "Message" (custom URL with UTM parameter).

Audience Targeting

  • In-market audiences: "Accommodation (hotels, resorts, etc.)," "Vacation homes and rentals," "Tours and outdoor activities."
  • Custom intent audiences: People searching for "Costa Rica resorts," "family vacation Costa Rica," "luxury spa retreats."
  • Demographic: 25–65, HH income $75K+.

Conversion Tracking

  • Use Google Ads conversion tracking (same conversion imported from GA4 or SynXis). Parameter: gclid (auto-added by Google Ads).
  • For the DM path, set landing page link to include a DM CTA; track clicks (secondary metric).

Bidding

  • Objective: Conversions (booking) or App installs (if you have an app).
  • Bid strategy: Target cost per action (CPA). Start at $45–60 per booking.

YouTube Long-form (In-stream & In-feed)

Campaign Structure

Create a separate campaign from YouTube Shorts, titled "YouTube Long-form" or "YouTube TrueView." This allows you to separately manage skippable in-stream ads (pre-roll/mid-roll) and in-feed ads (feed carousel).

Creative Setup

Skippable In-stream (TrueView)

  • Format: 16:9 horizontal, 15–300 seconds (viewers can skip after 5 seconds).
  • Hook: First 5 seconds are non-skippable and critical. Lead with a visual promise or question. Example: "Imagine waking up to sloths outside your window" (text overlay + footage of sloths on property).
  • Messaging: Frames 0–5s: hook. Frames 5–15s: property features (pool, spa, family activities). Frames 15–end: social proof (reviews, testimonials) + CTA.
  • CTA overlay: Appears at 8+ seconds, clickable banner at bottom of screen.

Non-skippable In-stream

  • Format: 16:9, max 20 seconds (YouTube policy; viewers cannot skip).
  • Impact: Use for remarketing only (to warm audiences who have visited your site). Standard audiences may resent being forced to watch.

In-feed Video Ads

  • Format: 16:9 or 4:3, static thumbnail image (1280x720px) plus 12-second video preview.
  • Placement: YouTube feed (right sidebar on desktop, below videos on mobile).
  • CTR note: In-feed relies on static thumbnail image quality. Test 2–3 thumbnail variants (beauty shot of property, sloth close-up, family moment).

Audience Targeting

  • In-market audiences (same as Shorts, above).
  • Remarketing: past website visitors (30-day window), YouTube channel subscribers, video viewers.
  • Lookalike audiences: built from past bookers' email list.

Conversion Tracking

  • Standard Google Ads conversion (SynXis booking event imported into GA4, then to Google Ads).
  • For DM path: use a landing page URL with a visible "Message Us" CTA, track clicks as secondary conversion.

Bidding

  • In-stream: Optimize for conversions (bookings). Target CPA: $50–75.
  • In-feed: Optimize for clicks (CTR); use lower bid cap ($1–3) since in-feed is higher-funnel awareness.

A/B Testing Structure

Landing Page Path vs. DM Path Split

Allocation

  • 50% budget → dedicated landing pages (specific property page or a comparison page).
  • 50% budget → direct message (DM CTA on ad or landing page with prominent message button).

Landing Page Path Metrics

  • Cost per click (CPC).
  • Landing page conversion rate (visitors who completed a booking inquiry or booking).
  • Cost per lead (CPL).
  • Cost per booking (CPB).
  • Average booking value (from SynXis data).

DM Path Metrics

  • Cost per message received (CPM sent = cost ÷ message starts).
  • Message reply rate (conversations that received a human response).
  • Cost per reply (cost ÷ number of replies from followers).
  • Booking conversion from DM (number of DM conversations that resulted in a booking, tracked via CRM or manual flagging).

Comparison Framework

Metric Landing Page DM What It Means
Cost per booking Direct from GA4 + SynXis Manual: (budget spent) ÷ (bookings sourced from DM that month) Which path drives cheaper confirmed bookings?
Lead quality Conversion rate (form submits ÷ landing page sessions) Reply rate (replied messages ÷ message starts) Which path attracts more engaged prospects?
Booking velocity Time from click to booking (GA4 cohort analysis) Time from first message to booking (CRM data) Which path closes faster?
Customer AOV Average booking value (SynXis) of LPO-sourced bookings Average booking value of DM-sourced bookings Which path attracts higher-value guests?

Measurement & Optimization Cadence

Weekly (During Ramp-up)

  • Check cost per result (landing page CPC, DM cost per message).
  • Flag any ad set with CPC 2x+ higher than average; pause and reallocate budget to winners.
  • Verify conversion tracking is firing correctly (check GA4 events, SynXis backend).

Bi-weekly (Ongoing)

  • Compare cost per booking between landing page and DM paths. Allocate more budget to the cheaper path.
  • Analyze creative performance: which visual hook (sloth vs. property) drives lower CPC?
  • Review DM response rate: are replies coming in? Are they converting to bookings?

Monthly (Strategy Review)

  • Aggregate revenue from both paths (landing page + DM sourced bookings).
  • Calculate ROAS: (revenue from paid social) ÷ (total spend).
  • Compare booking AOV across both paths. Is one path attracting higher-value guests?
  • Document winning creative variants and audience segments. Double down on them in the next campaign cycle.
  • Identify underperforming platforms (if YouTube long-form ROAS is weak, reduce allocation; if TikTok is strong, increase).

Data Plumbing: Conversion Tracking Setup

Critical: Conversion tracking must be working before you spend at scale. Test each conversion event in a staging environment first.

Landing Page Conversion (Booking Completed)

  • Source of truth: SynXis booking engine.
  • Flow: User lands on your property page → clicks "Check availability" → gets redirected to SynXis → completes booking.
  • Tracking method: GA4 event "booking_completed" fired by SynXis (may require dev work). Alternatively, use Google Ads conversion tracking on the SynXis success page (if SynXis allows custom pixel).
  • URL parameter: All landing page links include utm_source=paid_social&utm_medium=[platform]&utm_campaign=[property].
  • Verification: Book a test reservation from a test email, confirm the conversion fires in GA4.

DM Conversion (Message Sent)

  • Meta DM tracking: Meta Ads Manager automatically tracks "Conversation Started" (when a user clicks message and the chat window opens). You can optimize campaigns for this event.
  • TikTok DM workaround: No native DM destination. Use a landing page with a prominent DM CTA, then track link clicks and manually monitor incoming messages from TikTok traffic sources.
  • YouTube DM workaround: Same as TikTok; use a landing page with DM CTA.

Secondary: Booking Sourced from DM

  • Manual process: tag incoming DM conversations with the source (e.g., "DM - Facebook", "DM - TikTok").
  • Track in CRM or spreadsheet: which DM conversations converted to a booking, and on what date.
  • Calculate monthly: cost per DM booking = (monthly paid social budget allocated to DM path) ÷ (number of bookings sourced from DM that month).

Platform-by-Platform Rollout

Week 1: Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

  • Set up one campaign for Gardens (the lead property).
  • Create two ad sets: Landing Page variant, DM variant.
  • Add 2–3 creative variants to each.
  • Daily budget: $50 to start (allows $1,400 spend over 4 weeks for statistically valid results).
  • Monitor cost per result daily.

Week 2: YouTube Shorts + YouTube Long-form

  • Set up YouTube Shorts campaign (separate ad group).
  • Set up YouTube long-form campaign (in-stream TrueView + in-feed).
  • Daily budget per platform: $30 to start.
  • Verify conversion tracking is live before scaling.

Week 3: TikTok Ads

  • Set up TikTok Ads account (may require business registration if you haven't already).
  • Create campaign with Landing Page and DM ad groups.
  • Daily budget: $25 to start.
  • Expect 3–7 day learning phase (TikTok's algorithm optimizes automatically).

Week 4+: Consolidate & Scale

  • Compare cost per booking across all platforms.
  • Identify winners (cheapest cost per booking).
  • Reduce or pause underperformers.
  • Double the budget on the best-performing platform and messaging combination.

Key Assumptions & Risks

Assumption 1: Your SynXis booking engine can accept a direct referral parameter (e.g., source=facebook) so that bookings can be attributed to paid social source. If not, you'll need to implement an intermediate landing page that captures the source before redirecting to SynXis.
Assumption 2: GA4 is correctly tracking user IDs and conversions from your landing pages. If GA4 conversion events are not firing, landing page attribution will be blind. Verify this in a staging environment before scaling budget.
Assumption 3: Your DM inbox (Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM) can handle the inbound volume. Budget for staffing or a chatbot to respond to DM inquiries within 2 hours for optimal lead quality.
Assumption 4: The landing pages used in the landing page path are conversion-optimized (clear benefit statement, strong CTA, trust signals, mobile-responsive). If the landing page conversion rate is below 5%, fix the page before scaling budget.

Recommended Next Steps

  1. Verify conversion tracking. Test GA4 events and SynXis attribution with a test booking. Confirm that both landing page and DM conversions are trackable.
  2. Prepare creative assets. Export your existing vertical and horizontal video reels. If any need trimming or color correction, do that now.
  3. Set up landing pages. If you do not have dedicated property pages (Gardens family page, comparison page, etc.), create them using the structure described in the Gardens organic strategy briefing.
  4. Staff the DM queue. Assign a team member to monitor incoming messages on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok during business hours. Train them on your booking and inquiry process.
  5. Launch Week 1 (Meta). Start with Meta Ads, measure for 1–2 weeks, then layer in YouTube and TikTok.
