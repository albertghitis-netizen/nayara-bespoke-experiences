Fly into San José (SJO) or Liberia (LIR) international airports. Both receive direct flights from major US and European cities.

Take a short domestic flight from SJO to La Fortuna/Arenal (~30 minutes). The hotel can arrange transfers from the local airstrip.

Arrange a private transfer from San José (~3 hours) or Liberia (~2.5 hours). Scenic drive through the Central Valley with volcano views.

Rent a car at either airport and drive to Arenal. Well-paved roads with clear signage. The journey from SJO takes approximately 3 hours.

Over the past two decades, Nayara has reforested more than 600 acres of degraded pastureland, creating wildlife corridors that allow jaguars, tapirs, and scarlet macaws to move freely between protected areas.
