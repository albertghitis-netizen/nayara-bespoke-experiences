Domestic Flight to Bocas

Take a short domestic flight from Panama City to Bocas del Toro (BOC) , approximately 1 hour. Air Panama operates daily flights.

Private Boat Transfer

From Bocas Town, a private boat transfer brings you directly to the resort’s dock on Isla Pastores. The scenic ride takes approximately 20 minutes.
