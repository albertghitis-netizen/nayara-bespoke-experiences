Besame Mucho brings passionate Latin flavors to the heart of the Arenal rainforest at Nayara Springs. The restaurant celebrates the bold, soulful cooking traditions of Latin America — from slow-braised meats to vibrant ceviches — all prepared with ingredients from our organic gardens and local farms.

The name means 'kiss me a lot' — and the dining experience lives up to its promise. Every plate is crafted with love and fire, every cocktail shaken with tropical passion. The volcano-view terrace sets the stage for evenings that linger long after the last course, where the warmth of the food matches the warmth of the volcanic springs below.
