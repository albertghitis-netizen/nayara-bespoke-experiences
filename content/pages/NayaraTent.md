Immersed in the Canopy

Immerse yourself in the tranquil haven of your generously sized Nayara Tent, where opulence and nature intertwine seamlessly. Elevated on stilts above the rainforest floor, your tent becomes a private observatory for the symphony of bird songs and the vibrant wildlife that calls this jungle home.

Your Private Hot Spring Pool

Awaken your senses as the crystal-clear waters of your private pool, nourished by nearby hot springs, embrace your skin, leaving you refreshed and rejuvenated. Watch the Arenal Volcano emerge from morning mist while soaking in mineral-rich waters heated by the earth itself.
